# Keyword research: rental income tax calculator

Researched on 13 September 2026 in Semrush (United Kingdom database, desktop), with the live pages of the top competitors checked in Chrome. Volumes are Semrush's monthly UK estimates. Keyword difficulty (KD) differs slightly between Semrush reports, so a range is shown where two reports disagreed.

## Summary

- **Primary keyword: rental income tax calculator.** 1,300 searches a month, KD 25% (easy), CPC $1.22, informational and commercial intent. It has 3,400 searches worldwide.
- **Google treats about 25 calculator phrasings as one topic.** Taxfix's single calculator page ranks first for "rental income tax calculator", "landlord tax calculator", "uk rental income tax calculator", "buy to let tax calculator" and 260 other keywords. So one page should target the whole group, not one page per phrase.
- **Low authority can rank here.** checkmytax.uk has an Authority Score of 9 and 119 backlinks, yet ranks third for most of the group. Its advantage is a long, focused page.
- **The calculator group totals about 9,500 searches a month**, almost all with KD under 30%.
- **The biggest informational term is "tax on rental income"** (1,900, KD 24%). Guides rank for it, not calculators, so it now has its own guide page at `/tax-on-rental-income/`.

## Off-page: the remaining gap

The on-page gap is now closed. What separates positions 4 to 10 from position 1 is links. checkmytax.uk shows that about a hundred relevant backlinks can reach the top three for this group. After launch:

1. Verify the site in Google Search Console and submit `sitemap.xml`.
2. Ask for links where landlords already look:
   - landlord associations and local landlord forums;
   - letting agents' resources pages;
   - accountants who serve landlords;
   - property investment blogs and newsletters.
3. List the calculator in UK finance and landlord tool round-ups.
4. Track the calculator group and "tax on rental income" in a Semrush Position Tracking project on your own account. The shared account's projects belong to other people.
5. Review in 8 to 12 weeks. If "landlord tax calculator" lags "rental income tax calculator", add it to the calculator page's H2s.
