Landlord tax services

We help landlords with the tax on UK rental income by preparing and filing Self Assessment tax returns, including the UK property pages. If you would rather work out your own figures, the calculator and guides on this site stay free.

The help we offer

For most landlords, the tax on rent comes down to one document: the Self Assessment tax return. The rent you received, the costs you claim, the mortgage interest credit and any payments on account all run through it. Our services focus on getting that return right and filed on time.

What we do

We work from your records to prepare the UK property pages of your return. We check which of your costs are allowable, enter mortgage interest so the Section 24 credit is applied, and file the return with HMRC. Where they apply, we compare claiming your expenses with the property allowance or Rent a Room relief, so you can see which leaves you with less tax.

Who our services suit

Our services suit landlords filing a tax return for rental income for the first time, landlords with a mortgage, people who own property jointly, and anyone who would rather hand over the paperwork. They are for individuals who report rental income through Self Assessment. Companies that let property pay Corporation Tax instead, so they are outside these services.

How this fits with the free tools

The rental income tax calculator and the guides show how your tax is worked out, with no sign-up. You can use them to check an estimate before you contact us, or to decide you are happy to file your own return. The guide to your first landlord tax return is a good place to start if you have not filed before.
