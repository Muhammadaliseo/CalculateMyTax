Give your email a clear subject line and tell us which page or tool it is about. The notes below explain what else helps us give you a useful answer.

What to include in your email

Questions about the calculator

Tell us the tax year and region you chose, and what you expected to see. To show us your figures, use "Copy shareable link" in the calculator and paste the link into your email. Your figures are written into that link, so only send it if you are happy for us to see them.

Reporting an error

Send us the address of the page, the figure or sentence you think is wrong, and a link to the GOV.UK, HMRC or gov.scot page that shows the correct position if you have one.

Asking about our Self Assessment service

Tell us briefly what you would like help with, such as how many properties you let and whether you have filed a Self Assessment tax return before. You can read about the Self Assessment tax return service before you get in touch.

Keep sensitive details out of your email

Please do not email us your bank details, your National Insurance number, your Unique Taxpayer Reference or copies of tax documents. Email is not a secure way to send them. Our privacy policy explains how we handle the emails you send.
