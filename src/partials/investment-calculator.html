Longer-term illustration

Illustrated total profit before income tax and Capital Gains Tax: —. Money left after selling costs and paying off the mortgage: —.

Total profit is the cash flow added up over the years, before income tax, plus what is left after a sale, less the cash you put in. It is not the same as the after-tax figure at the top, and no tax is estimated for future years. The interest rate stays the same throughout, and remortgaging, early repayment charges, inflation and tax on a sale are all left out.

Equity year by year: the property's value less the mortgage still owed. Negative equity shows in the table rather than as a bar.
