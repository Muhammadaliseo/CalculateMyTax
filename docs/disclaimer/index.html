Disclaimer

All figures on CalculateMyTax are estimates and are not tax advice. They are based on published HMRC rates and the figures you enter, and they are not a substitute for professional advice.

Last updated 13 September 2026

How our figures are worked out

The calculator, rate tables and worked examples on this site use published HMRC rates, last verified on 13 September 2026. Rates and rules can change, and an estimate is only as accurate as the figures you put in.

What the calculator does not cover

The calculator does not cover:

  • property owned through a limited company
  • non-resident landlords
  • overseas property
  • Capital Gains Tax
  • savings and dividend income
  • pension contributions
  • Gift Aid

If any of these apply to you, your tax could be different from our estimate.

Future tax years

Estimates for future tax years may include assumed figures where Scottish or Welsh rates have not yet been set. These estimates may change once the rates are confirmed.

Our liability

To the extent the law allows, we do not accept liability for decisions made using this site. Our terms of use explain the conditions for using it.

Check before you act

Check your position with HMRC or a qualified tax adviser before you file a tax return or make a financial decision. You can find ways to get in touch with HMRC on GOV.UK: Contact HMRC.
